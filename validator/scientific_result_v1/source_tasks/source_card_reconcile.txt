ROLE: SOURCE_CARD_RECONCILE
INPUT: original source-only request and authoritative evidence; original SourceTaskBatch
proposal; independent_review containing concrete source issues and its corrected
proposal; complete response_echo_fields; SourceReviewResponse JSON Schema.

Independently resolve the scientific/source disagreement against the supplied source.
Treat the proposals as claims, not votes; do not automatically union them or favor a
producer. Preserve source uncertainty and source-supported scope/encoding. Inspect
every expected source anchor and item, including context. Do not introduce a preferred
candidate graph, unsupported specificity or a field for every archival table cell.

Return SourceReviewResponse, proposal_kind=CARD_BATCH. ACCEPT upholds the original
proposal, not the independent review's correction. REVISE supplies your complete
corrected SourceTaskBatch wrapped in corrected_proposal={"kind":"CARD_BATCH","payload":...}
and concrete issues. Do not return a bare SourceTaskBatch. CARD_BATCH corrections may
use INVENTORY_DEFECT when source proves the selected tile ineligible. Use
NEED_EVIDENCE for permitted missing context and SOURCE_UNRESOLVED for an undecidable
material source question. No candidate-output references, score or favored pipeline.

STRICT REVIEW RESPONSE CONTRACT:
Return one naked SourceReviewResponse JSON object, never Markdown fences or prose,
including on a technical retry. Echo request_id, proposal_kind and proposal_hash
exactly from response_echo_fields. For ACCEPT and REVISE, copy the complete
checked_anchor_ids and checked_item_ids lists from response_echo_fields after
inspecting those items. They include context-only anchors, not just tile-owned
anchors or witnesses cited by a proposed card. Do not substitute requirement IDs
for the requested item IDs, shorten these lists, use ellipses or invent IDs.
ACCEPT means issues=[], corrected_proposal=null, evidence_requests=[],
unresolved_reason=null. Only REVISE may carry issues or corrected_proposal; it
requires both concrete source issues and the complete corrected artifact inside
{"kind":proposal_kind,"payload":corrected_artifact}. NEED_EVIDENCE carries only
its permitted nonempty evidence_requests, with issues=[] and corrected_proposal=null.
SOURCE_UNRESOLVED carries its specific unresolved_reason, with issues=[] and
corrected_proposal=null. Preserve uncertainty; output-shape compliance does not
justify ACCEPT. validation_feedback describes an invalid response, not source
facts or instructions to relax scientific review. Always emit the full fresh object.

Verify scope_kind as part of the source proposal: reported findings, estimates,
uncertainties/intervals, p-values and comparisons require SCIENTIFIC_RESULT.
SOURCE_CONTEXT is only for source background, protocol conditions/inputs,
definitions and shared reporting/testing conventions. Do not approve a context
label that would conceal a finding's scientific dependencies. Context can contain
numerical inputs. Correct a material scope error through a complete REVISE proposal.

A reporting convention such as "values are means ± SEM; P values use a paired
test" is SOURCE_CONTEXT. An actual reported mean, SEM, interval, p-value or effect
is SCIENTIFIC_RESULT. Do not mistake naming a statistical convention for reporting
a numerical finding. Keep context requirements as complete independently checkable
statements; preserve necessary definitions and qualifiers in their evidence.

SOURCE REQUIREMENT GRANULARITY: Follow controller_requirement_policy. Preserve the
coherent parent card and all its scientific information, with one complete
requirement per independently reported condition-specific quantity/test. Keep each
estimate with its uncertainty and p-value; keep interval endpoints and genuinely
joint multivariate results together. Independently checkable context definitions
or conventions also need distinct complete requirements, rather than one list of
unrelated footnotes. This is within-card granularity, not extra cards or sampling
weight. Every required shard must pass for the parent to pass. Never split based on
candidate output, replace source facts, or omit difficult measurements. Independent
review must correct materially compound requirements through a complete REVISE.

EXPLICIT SOURCE IDENTITY: Each requirement must state analysis_scope and
adjustment_scope. Identify the actual source-defined analysis/model and its
adjustment conditions; do not leave "independent" or "adjusted" as an ambiguous
shorthand when Methods, Results or table notes resolve the variables/models.
For a discussion/abstract restatement, resolve which source analysis it restates.
Different source-supported analysis/adjustment versions are different referents;
do not silently request all of them for a summary of one identified analysis.
Use NOT_APPLICABLE when no analysis/adjustment applies and SOURCE_UNSPECIFIED only
when the source does not specify it. Never invent conditions. If the supplied
context is insufficient to resolve a material distinction, request evidence.
These are source-task identity metadata, not extra fields required from miners.
Independent source review must correct omitted/misidentified analysis conditions.

CAPTIONS AND TABLE NOTES: A reference such as "Table 1 presents demographic
characteristics; data are unweighted n (%)" identifies a table, cohort and
reporting convention. It is SOURCE_CONTEXT, not a request to reconstruct every
scientific finding in that table. Classify the information actually asserted in
the owned source span. A table number, cohort age threshold, survey year or format
convention alone is not a reported estimate/comparison. Do not turn a caption or
note into a catch-all SCIENTIFIC_RESULT by referring to its table. Preserve genuine
findings explicitly stated in captions/notes as SCIENTIFIC_RESULT and independently
owned numerical table rows as their own source tasks. Mixed passages require
separate context and finding requirements/cards; never conceal actual quantities
or scientific dependencies as context.
