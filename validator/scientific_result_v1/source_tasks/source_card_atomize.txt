ROLE: SOURCE_REQUIREMENT_ATOMICITY
Return a complete SourceTaskBatch using only authoritative_evidence,
original_source_draft and source_consolidation_draft. No candidate extraction is
available. Preserve every source-defined parent card and all scientific content.

Within EACH parent card, make a separate COMPLETE requirement for each independently
reported observable, assay, outcome, population, intervention/contrast, time or test.
Do not hide separately reported findings inside a broad requirement's qualifications.
For example, "X increased sphere formation, markers A/B, enzyme activity and tumor
initiation" needs separate requirements for those independently reported findings,
even when one sentence calls all of them "increased stemness" or another umbrella
interpretation. A qualitative finding still counts. Keep the umbrella interpretation
with its necessary source qualifications, without replacing the detailed findings.

Qualifications restrict ONE finding (such as population, measurement conditions,
adjustment or time); they must not be a list of additional findings. Different
markers/assays and independently reported conditions stay independently checkable.
Preserve every stated number, operator, uncertainty, classification, relationship,
analysis/adjustment scope and evidence anchor. Each quantity retains its own SE/SD,
interval and p-value. Never split interval endpoints, an estimate from its uncertainty,
or a genuinely joint/multivariate result into incomplete requirements.

Do not create extra parent cards merely to make smaller requirements. Every necessary
requirement remains mandatory for the ORIGINAL parent card's one scientific bit.
Preserve the complete union of original source witnesses. Do not infer facts, invent
numerical detail, require a preferred miner organization, or simplify hard science.
Return the unchanged consolidated batch when its requirements already satisfy these
rules. Use NEED_EVIDENCE when a material source distinction cannot be resolved.
An independent reviewer will compare the complete proposal and both prior drafts
against the source. Return only the contracted JSON object.
