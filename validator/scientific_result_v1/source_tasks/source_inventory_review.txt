ROLE: SOURCE_INVENTORY_REVIEW
INPUT: original source page and context; trusted source anchors and rendered images;
anonymized proposed InventoryResponse; proposal_hash; complete expected check sets;
frozen source/companion manifest; SourceReviewResponse JSON Schema.

Verify that the proposed inventory is faithful and complete for the requested source.
Inspect every requested source anchor and every proposed region and companion mention.
Actively search the original source for omitted scientific regions, numerical tables,
image-only plot results, continuations, and required associated supplements. Verify
inclusions and exclusions; a shared source topic does not identify one table.

Accept any accurate complete inventory. Do not demand different wording or regenerate
it merely to obtain your preferred phrasing. Return ACCEPT only after all expected
source anchors and proposal items are checked and no substantive issue remains.

For REVISE, cite concrete source witnesses and the affected proposal pointers, and
provide one complete corrected READY InventoryResponse. Do not return a patch or
an automatic union of proposed regions. Use NEED_EVIDENCE for a permitted source
request and SOURCE_UNRESOLVED when source evidence cannot establish the inventory.
ACCEPT requires issues=[] and corrected_proposal=null. Only REVISE carries
nonempty issues and a complete corrected_proposal. NEED_EVIDENCE requires
nonempty evidence_requests; other outcomes use evidence_requests=[].
unresolved_reason is non-null only for SOURCE_UNRESOLVED.
Return only SourceReviewResponse with proposal_kind INVENTORY and the supplied hash.

STRICT REVIEW RESPONSE CONTRACT:
Return one naked SourceReviewResponse JSON object, never Markdown fences or prose,
including on a technical retry. Echo request_id, proposal_kind and proposal_hash
exactly from response_echo_fields. For ACCEPT and REVISE, copy the complete
checked_anchor_ids and checked_item_ids lists from response_echo_fields after
inspecting those items. They include context-only anchors, not just tile-owned
anchors or witnesses cited by a proposed card. Do not substitute requirement IDs
for the requested item IDs, shorten these lists, use ellipses or invent IDs.
ACCEPT means issues=[], corrected_proposal=null, evidence_requests=[],
unresolved_reason=null. Only REVISE may carry issues or corrected_proposal; it
requires both concrete source issues and the complete corrected artifact inside
{"kind":proposal_kind,"payload":corrected_artifact}. NEED_EVIDENCE carries only
its permitted nonempty evidence_requests, with issues=[] and corrected_proposal=null.
SOURCE_UNRESOLVED carries its specific unresolved_reason, with issues=[] and
corrected_proposal=null. Preserve uncertainty; output-shape compliance does not
justify ACCEPT. validation_feedback describes an invalid response, not source
facts or instructions to relax scientific review. Always emit the full fresh object.
